Our story

Turning spaces into stories.

Pro Realtys advises with the rigour of a professional practice rather than the opportunism of a broker. Every recommendation is grounded in genuine market analysis, statistical benchmarking, and complete legal verification — from title deed to encumbrance certificate.

The practice spans the full property lifecycle: advisory, development, investment, and asset management — delivering transparency and a fair, well-substantiated outcome for every client.

Pro Realtys describes itself as a third-generation real estate practice in Chennai, operating in alignment with the Real Estate Regulation and Development framework.

Pro Realtys is part of Swarna Sudarshan Group, alongside Pro Hotel in hospitality and Pro Events.

The heritage and generation figures above are Pro Realtys’ own, as published on prorealtys.in. No founding year or incorporation record has been supplied to this site, so they are attributed rather than stated.
