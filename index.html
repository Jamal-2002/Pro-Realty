The project

Forty-seven plots, one approved plan.

Sri Sakthi Nagar Extension is a CMDA-approved layout of house sites in Varadharajapuram, on the Mudichur side of Tambaram. Every plot in it is numbered and sized on record, approved block by block under planning permits 0005/08 to 0025/08.

Land in an approved layout is finite by definition: forty-seven plots were drawn, and no more will be added. Twelve are already allotted. What remains is what remains.

“Verified first. Introduced second.”

This is plotted land — plots and planned internal roads. Nothing on this site claims an amenity, a clubhouse or a facility, because none is part of what is being sold.

Location & connectivity

Picture the ordinary Tuesday. That is the real test of an address.

Great land is not about a view from a brochure — it is about the drive you make two hundred times a year. Sri Sakthi Nagar Extension sits in Varadharajapuram, on the Mudichur side of Tambaram: schools, a college, two hospitals and the corporation office are all within a short radius, and GST Road, the bypass and Tambaram railway station tie you to the rest of Chennai.

Questions

What buyers ask us.

Answered only where the approved documents answer them. Where they do not, we would rather leave the question off this page than write a comfortable guess.

Is the layout approved?
Yes. Sri Sakthi Nagar Extension is a CMDA-approved layout of house sites, approved block by block under planning permits P.P. 0005/08 to 0025/08. The permit number for every individual plot is listed in the layout’s area statement and is shown on each plot’s page on this site.
How many plots are there?
Forty-seven, totalling 87,153 sq.ft. The layout is a fixed, approved plan — no further plots will be added to it.
How many plots are still available?
Thirty-five plots totalling 67,411 sq.ft are shown as available; twelve are allotted. Availability is shown per plot in the interactive layout, with the date it was last verified.
What plot sizes are available?
From 577 sq.ft to 3,645 sq.ft. The available plots group into three bands: Compact (577–1,500 sq.ft, 8 plots), Family (1,501–2,100 sq.ft, 18 plots) and Estate (2,101–3,645 sq.ft, 9 plots).
How wide are the roads?
The layout has planned internal roads 24 feet and 20 feet wide, with existing 24-foot roads along the south and west edges of the site.
What facings are available?
Among the available plots: 13 east-facing, 15 west, 3 north-east, 3 south-east and 1 north. Each plot’s facing is recorded in the approved area statement and shown in the interactive layout.
Where exactly is the layout?
Varadharajapuram, on the Mudichur side of Tambaram, Chennai. The approved layout is recorded as being in Varadharajapuram Village, Sriperumbudur Taluk, Kancheepuram District.
What is nearby?
Vels Global School is 650 m away and SSP Vidhyashram 1.9 km; TMG College of Arts and Science is 700 m and Dhanalakshmi College of Engineering, Tambaram is 5 km; Sri Sakthi Hospital is 1.2 km and Annai Arul Hospital 3.6 km; the TNEB office at Tambaram Corporation is 400 m and Varadharajapuram Toll Plaza 3 km. These are approximate road distances from the layout.
What documents will I be shown?
Pro Realty’s states that title is traced and the encumbrance certificate reviewed end to end before any property is introduced, and that pricing is benchmarked against comparable sales and guideline value. Documentation and banking are supported through to registration. The documents themselves are shown to you at the site visit.
How do I see the plots?
Call +91 755 008 5555 and arrange a site visit. The brochure describes the process as: call, visit and walk the roads, have title and encumbrance shown to you before you decide, and then register with documentation and banking supported to completion.
Can I get a loan?
Pro Realty’s states that documentation and banking are supported through to registration.
Can I enquire about one specific plot?
Yes. Select any plot in the interactive layout and the enquiry carries that plot’s number, area and facing with it. Each plot also has its own page that can be shared.
