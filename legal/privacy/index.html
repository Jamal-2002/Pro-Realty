Privacy

What we do with your details.

What we collect

Only what you type into an enquiry form: your name, your phone number, and optionally your email address, a preferred site-visit date and time, the plot you are asking about, and a message. We also record which page the enquiry came from.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry and arrange a site visit. When you submit a form you tick a box that says: “I agree to be contacted by Pro Realtys about Sri Sakthi Nagar Extension by phone, WhatsApp or email.” We record which version of that wording you saw.

What we do not do

We do not sell your details, and we do not share them with third parties other than the service that delivers the enquiry to us. This site does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Technical detail

To limit automated abuse we store a one-way hash of the network address an enquiry came from. The address itself is not stored and the hash cannot be reversed to it.

Correcting or removing your details

Call +91 755 008 5555 or write to info@swarnasudarshan.com and we will correct or delete what we hold.

This notice describes the data this website actually collects. Retention periods and the identity of the data fiduciary are to be confirmed with the client before launch and this page updated accordingly.
